About Pampered Paws

Since 2009, Pampered Paws has been the premier destination for luxury pet grooming and wellness. Our certified groomers combine expertise with genuine love for animals, ensuring every pet receives the highest standard of care.

We believe every pet deserves to feel pampered and loved. From basic grooming to full spa treatments, our state-of-the-art facility offers comprehensive services in a calm, stress-free environment.
